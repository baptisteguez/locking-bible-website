Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 · Applies to Lumi Bible 2.0 and later

In short: Lumi Bible has no account and no login. Your first name, your answers, your likes, favorites, streak and reading stay on your iPhone.
  • To improve the app, we use Amplitude to measure how the app is used (for example, which onboarding step people reach). This data is not linked to your name or identity.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

1. Who we are

Lumi Bible (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an iOS app that offers a feed of Bible verses, the complete Bible offline, and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Lumi Bible was previously called Locking Bible. This website, lockingbible.com, is the app's official website.

The app is published by Baptiste Guez, who is responsible for the processing described in this policy. You can reach us at contact@lockingbible.com.

2. What stays on your device

Lumi Bible works without an account. To give you a personal experience, the app keeps the following information only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage (shared with the Lumi Bible widgets through an App Group container on the same device):

  • your first name, if you enter it during onboarding, used to greet you in the app;
  • the answers you give during onboarding (for example your age range, your reading habits, your goals, your estimated screen time) and the reading plan built from them;
  • the verses you like, your favorites, your streak, the days you opened a verse, your points and badges;
  • your reading position in the Bible, text size and reading colors;
  • your language, background, brightness, app icon, reminder and widget settings;
  • whether you have an active Premium subscription, so the widgets can show the right content.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you delete the app. If your iPhone backups are enabled, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, under Apple's control.

The complete Bible, the verses and the widgets content are built into the app and work offline.

3. Usage analytics (Amplitude)

To understand how the app is used and make it better, Lumi Bible uses Amplitude, an analytics service provided by Amplitude, Inc. (United States). Analytics run in the app only; the widgets send nothing.

What is collected

DataExamples
Product interactionApp opens and sessions, screens viewed, onboarding steps completed (step number and name), whether the onboarding ended with a subscription, when the subscription screen is closed without subscribing, and when a subscription or free trial starts (plan, price, currency, trial or not, where it started).
Device identifierA random identifier generated for analytics and Apple's identifier for vendor (IDFV), used to count unique devices. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
Technical informationDevice model, iOS version, app version, mobile carrier, language and region settings.
Approximate locationAmplitude may derive an approximate location (country, region or city) from your IP address. Lumi Bible never accesses your precise location.

What is never collected

Your first name, your onboarding answers, the verses you read, like or save, your favorites, and anything you share are never sent to Amplitude or to us.

How it is used

Analytics data is used only to measure and improve the app (for example, to see where people stop during onboarding). It is not linked to your identity, not used for advertising, not sold, and not combined with data from other companies for tracking.

Amplitude processes this data on our behalf as a service provider, under its own security commitments. See Amplitude's privacy notice.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Lumi Bible Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple's App Store (StoreKit).

  • Payments and billing are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your card or banking details, your Apple Account email or your name.
  • The app checks with Apple, on your device, whether your subscription is active.
  • When a subscription or free trial starts, the analytics event described in section 3 records the plan and price, without any payment details.

Apple processes your purchase information under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.

5. Permissions

Lumi Bible only asks for permissions when a feature needs them. You can change them anytime in iOS Settings.

  • Notifications (optional): verse reminders at the times you choose, and a reminder one day before a free trial ends. They are scheduled and delivered on your iPhone by iOS; there is no push server.
  • Add to Photos (optional): only to save the image of a verse you choose to share. The app cannot read your photo library.

Lumi Bible does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, health data or precise location.

The app may also show Apple's standard rating prompt. Ratings are handled by Apple and we do not receive your identity.

6. Screen Time (previous versions)

Earlier versions of the app, published as Locking Bible, used Apple's Screen Time technology to pause selected apps. This feature has been removed in Lumi Bible 2.0. The app no longer requests or uses Screen Time access (Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity), and never uploaded your app selections.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. Information is only handled by:

  • Amplitude, for the analytics described in section 3;
  • Apple, for purchases, notifications, widgets and system features, under Apple's own policies;
  • authorities, if required by law.

Lumi Bible does not contain advertising and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency.

8. Contacting us

If you email contact@lockingbible.com, we receive your email address and what you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and keep a record of the conversation as long as reasonably necessary.

9. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Analytics data is kept only as long as it is useful to improve the app, and then deleted or aggregated. Because it is not linked to your identity, we may need your help to find it: contact us and we will do our best to delete the data associated with your device.
  • Support emails are deleted when no longer needed, or on request.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Brazil or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. To exercise these rights, email contact@lockingbible.com.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Usage analytics: our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app, limited to data that is not linked to your identity. You can object at any time by contacting us.
  • Subscriptions: performance of the contract you enter into when you subscribe.
  • Support emails: our legitimate interest in answering you.

International transfers

Amplitude may process analytics data in the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

California

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

11. Children

Lumi Bible is not directed at children under 13 and has no social features or advertising. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Your personal content stays on your iPhone, protected by iOS security (device encryption and app sandboxing). Analytics data is sent over encrypted connections. No method is perfectly secure, but we limit what leaves your device to what is described above.

13. This website

lockingbible.com does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. Our hosting provider may process basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) in short-lived server logs to deliver pages securely. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

14. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date. If a change significantly affects how your information is handled, we will make it clear here before it takes effect.

15. Contact

Questions about privacy or a request about your data: contact@lockingbible.com
