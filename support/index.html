Updating from Locking Bible

Why is the app now called Lumi Bible?

Lumi Bible 2.0 is a complete redesign of Locking Bible. It now focuses on Scripture itself: a feed of verses, the complete Bible offline, widgets, streaks and badges. Your subscription carries over automatically.

Where is the app-blocking feature?

Pausing other apps with Screen Time has been removed in version 2.0. Lumi Bible no longer asks for Screen Time access.

Some apps are still blocked after the update

If protection was on when you updated, iOS may keep the old block in place. To remove it, open Settings → Screen Time, find Lumi Bible (or Locking Bible) in the list of apps with Screen Time access and turn it off.

You can also delete Lumi Bible and reinstall it from the App Store. Your subscription is linked to your Apple Account and can be restored for free. If anything stays blocked, write to us and we'll help.

Subscription

What is free, and what is Premium?

Free: the complete Bible, 15 new verses a day in the feed, every widget, streaks, badges and reminders, and the background and icon you choose during setup.

Premium: unlimited verses, Words of Jesus, Did you know?, all 24 backgrounds with brightness control, and all 10 app icons.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Lumi Bible, tap the profile icon (top right), then Restore purchases in the Settings section. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Lumi Bible, then tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or trial) to avoid being charged for the next one.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Using Lumi Bible

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, then search for Lumi Bible. Choose Bible Verse, Verse of the Day or My Streak, and a size. To pick the category of the Bible Verse widget, touch and hold it, then tap Edit Widget. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add widgets under the clock.

How do I change the language or the Bible translation?

Tap the profile icon (top right), then Language. Each language comes with its own translation: King James Version (English), Louis Segond 1910 (French), Reina-Valera 1909 (Spanish) and Bíblia Livre (Brazilian Portuguese). “Automatic” follows your iPhone's language.

How do I change my reminders?

Tap the profile icon (top right), then Notifications, and choose 1, 3 or 5 reminders a day and their times. If notifications were turned off, enable them in iOS Settings → Notifications → Lumi Bible.

How do badges work?

Every verse you like or save to favorites earns 1 point. Points take you through eight levels, from Beginner to Theologian. Your streak counts the days in a row you open a verse.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. The Bible, the verses and the widgets work offline, and there is no account to create. Your likes, favorites and progress are stored on your iPhone, so they are not transferred if you delete the app or change phones.

Where do the verses and facts come from?

Verses come from public-domain Bible translations, and from the Bíblia Livre (CC BY 4.0) in Portuguese. Short verses in the feed and widgets may be exact excerpts of a longer verse; tap the reference to read it in full. The “Did you know?” facts are written for the app and checked against Scripture.
