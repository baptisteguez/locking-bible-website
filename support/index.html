To help us help you faster

If you’re reporting a problem, it helps to include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • what you were doing when the problem happened;
  • a screenshot, if you can.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through your App Store account.

  • Manage or cancel: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or Manage Subscription inside the app’s Settings.
  • Restore a purchase: open Locking Bible → Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • Refunds: refunds are issued by Apple — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Protection & Screen Time

  • Turn Protection on or off: in the app’s Settings.
  • Change protected apps: Settings → Protected Apps.
  • Revoke Screen Time access: iOS Settings → Screen Time.

Privacy
