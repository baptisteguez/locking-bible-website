Keep the Word in sight.

Add Lumi Bible to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, in small, medium or large. Light or dark, they follow your iPhone.

  • Verse of the Day — a new passage at midnight
  • My Streak — your daily rhythm at a glance
  • Lock Screen widgets — rectangular, inline and circular
